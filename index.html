LYRA

Bit-Perfect Audio Engine & Laboratory Reference Station

The Concept

LYRA is a premium, high-fidelity audio system designed for audiophiles and sound professionals who demand absolute bit-to-bit integrity. Operating with a custom, ultra-low latency hardware-direct audio abstraction layer, LYRA bypasses standard system mixing limitations to ensure standard-setting studio reference playback on mobile architecture.

  • ⚡ Engine: Bitperfect Audio Core v1.0 (Direct-Hardware Abstraction)
  • 💎 Integrity: Zero-resampling, zero-dithering, unadulterated signal path
  • 🎯 Monetization: One-time laboratory investment. Zero subscriptions. Zero ads.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the data processing practices of the LYRA application. To comply with Google Play Console policies, the legal terms are detailed below:

1. Introduction and Data Controller

LYRA operates under a strict offline-first, local-processing philosophy. The service is provided to ensure local, high-performance audio playback.

2. Information Collection and Use

LYRA does NOT collect, harvest, transmit, or store any personal data, user identifiers, device tracking codes, or personal information.

3. Local Storage and File Permissions

The Application requires local permission to access audio files stored on the device solely to parse metadata and stream data directly to the hardware. No files are sent to remote servers.

4. Google Play In-App Purchases

The Application utilizes the official Google Play Billing API. While billing is handled securely by Google, the Application only receives a local verification token to unlock features.
